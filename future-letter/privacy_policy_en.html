Future Letter Privacy Policy

Effective: November 17, 2026

At a glance
  • There are no accounts. Your letters and drafts are stored only on your device.
  • The app does not send the contents of your letters to the developer or to any server.
  • The app contains no ads, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • Letters leave the device only when you choose to export a backup file yourself.

1. Information we handle and why

Future Letter works without signing up or logging in. The app keeps the following information only on your device, and uses it solely so you can write a letter and read it again after the set time has passed.

  • The text of your letters and how you wrote them (free writing or answering a question)
  • Drafts you are still writing (saved automatically)
  • When a letter was sealed, when it opens, whether it has been read, and when it was first read
  • Whether the first-seal message has already been shown

The developer cannot access this information. The app has no feature that sends information to a server.

2. Notifications

To let you know when a letter opens, the app uses local notifications. They are scheduled on your device and do not pass through any external server. If notifications are turned off, the app does not seal the letter and asks you to turn them on instead. You can change the notification permission in your device settings at any time.

3. Backup and restore

If you choose "Export letters", your letters are packed into an encrypted backup file (.letterbak) and handed to the location or service you pick in your device's share sheet. If you choose "Import letters", the app reads letters from the backup file you select.

  • The backup file is encrypted so that its contents cannot be read outside the app, and the app rejects a file that has been altered.
  • The encryption is not protected by a personal password you choose, so please keep the file somewhere safe.
  • How a service you send the file to (for example cloud storage or a messaging app) handles it is governed by that service's own policy.

4. Third parties and external services

The developer does not provide any information, including your letters, to third parties. The app includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Fonts and every other file needed to display the app are included in the app itself and are not downloaded from elsewhere.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Once a letter is sealed it cannot be deleted inside the app, and it stays on your device.
  • Deleting the app removes the letters and drafts stored on the device.
  • Backup files you saved or shared yourself remain after you delete the app. Delete them from wherever you saved them.
  • On iOS, if you use a system backup such as iCloud device backup, your letters may be included in it. On Android, system automatic backup is turned off for this app.

6. Children

Because the app collects no information regardless of age, it does not separately collect or use children's personal information.

7. Your choices

All information is on your device, so you control it by managing the device and any backup files yourself. The developer holds none of your information, so there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, or delete on request.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new text and its effective date will be posted on this page.
